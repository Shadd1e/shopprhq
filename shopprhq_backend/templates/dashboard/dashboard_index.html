Getting started with ShopprHQ
1
Add your products
Go to Products and add everything you sell — name, price, and initial stock. You can also bulk upload via CSV.
2
Set your operator number
Go to Settings and set your operator phone number. This number will receive new order alerts and can confirm cash pickups by sending confirm ORDERCODE on WhatsApp.
3
Connect your bank account
Go to Settings → Connect Bank to add your account number. Card payments will settle directly to you. ShopprHQ takes 1%.
4
Connect your WhatsApp number
Message us the WhatsApp number your customers will use to order from you. We'll activate it within 24 hours.

⚠️ Important: The number must not be currently registered on the WhatsApp Business App. It must be a fresh number or one you're willing to migrate from the app.
5
You're live!
Once your number is connected, customers can message it on WhatsApp and order directly. Your dashboard will show all orders in real time.
